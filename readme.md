## 🏆 NIST Recognition & Evaluation

Viante.ai participates in the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Face Recognition Technology Evaluation (FRTE).

Our algorithms have published evaluation results in:

- FRTE 1:1 – Face Verification
- FRTE 1:N – Face Identification

Explore our official NIST evaluation records:

- [NIST FRTE 1:1 Results](https://pages.nist.gov/frvt/html/frvt11.html)
- [NIST FRTE 1:N Results](https://pages.nist.gov/frvt/html/frvt1N.html)
- [Viante.ai NIST Report Card](https://face.nist.gov/frte/reportcards/11/viante_003.html)
